About TC Sports Cards

A Hobby Shop Built by Collectors

TC Sports Cards is Zeeland's home base for sports card collectors — vintage and modern. From wax-pack relics to the newest rookie hits, the shop has been quietly stacking the case for years.

The Case

Walk in and you'll find thousands and thousands of singles, sealed boxes, supplies, vintage finds, and memorabilia. The case is too deep to put online — instead, the shop puts up a small, curated set of online exclusives every month. Sealed product, special repacks, mystery hits, and the kind of items that move fast.

Online Exclusives

Items listed on this site can only be purchased online. Pick them up at the counter or have them shipped. Every order earns rewards points. Climb the lineup and stack a multiplier.

Why Rewards?

Because the hobby is a long game. The longer you collect, the better your points work for you. Hall of Famer tier earns double — every dollar a permanent investment in your next break.

Stop In

The shop is open Mon–Fri 11–7, Sat 10–6. We're at 123 Main Avenue, Zeeland, MI 49464. Bring something to trade — we'll take a look.
